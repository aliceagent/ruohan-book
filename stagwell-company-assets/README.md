### Taglines / headlines
- Giving the Modern Marketer Access to the Best AI—Safely
- Your team’s always-on AI powerhouse
- Enterprise-grade access to major LLMs, plus the ability to create custom AI assistants
- Get your head in the Cloud *(parent Marketing Cloud line)*

### Short description
A secure, unified platform that gives brands and agencies one login, one contract, and one security standard for leading multimodal LLMs, image/video tools, and purpose-built marketing assistants — without juggling subscriptions. Client data is not used to train underlying models.

### Longer description (official)
The Marketing Cloud built Agent Cloud as a single, secure entry point to the world’s leading AI models. Rather than locking teams into one vendor, it provides access to the frontier of multimodal AI — LLMs plus image and video generation — under one security standard. Teams can also build and share custom AI assistants for creative, media, communications, and research, then deploy them across the organization.

### FAQ copy (schema.org, product page)
- **What is Agent Cloud?** A secure AI workspace for marketers to access top LLMs and custom-built AI assistants through a single login. It centralizes models and tools, standardizes governance, supports specialized tasks, and eliminates tool sprawl and unsanctioned AI use.
- **Which models?** Enterprise versions of ChatGPT, Claude Sonnet, Grok, Gemini Pro, plus image/video tools; switch models per task. Third-party integrations for synthetic audiences, social listening, and citation-backed research.
- **Data protection?** Uploaded data is never used to train LLMs. Role-based access and org policies. SOC 2 processes for security, availability, and confidentiality.
- **Pricing?** One annual base fee plus usage-based charges.

### Traction (Feb 2026 expansion PR)
- 25,000+ monthly agent interactions
- 500+ custom-built agents
- +30% monthly interactions / +25% daily engagement time since launch

## 3. The Targeting Machine (SATS)

**Public product name:** Stagwell Agentic Targeting System (**SATS**)  
**Also called:** The Targeting Machine (Stagwell earnings / Mark Penn); “holy grail of marketing”  
**Parent:** The Marketing Cloud + Palantir Foundry partnership  
**Category:** Privacy-first audience intelligence / agentic targeting

There is **no standalone targetingmachine.com**. The live product page is on The Marketing Cloud marketplace.

### URLs
- Product: https://www.themarketingcloud.com/marketplace/sats
- Palantir partnership: https://www.stagwellglobal.com/palantir-technologies-inc-pltr-and-stagwell-stgw-join-forces-to-design-product-for-the-future-of-marketing/
- Partnership annual-report page: https://www.stagwellglobal.com/2025-palantir-partnership/

### Taglines / headlines
- Enterprise-grade audience intelligence
- Privacy-first audience platform
- Build high-intent audiences from Stagwell proprietary data
- The holy grail of marketing *(Mark Penn)*

### Short description
Privacy-first audience platform that fuses a brand’s first-party data with the Stagwell ID Graph, third-party media data, and proprietary consumer intelligence — then lets marketers query, segment, and activate audiences conversationally on Palantir Foundry.

### Longer description (official)
SATS is an enterprise-grade, privacy-first audience intelligence platform built natively on Palantir Foundry. It integrates fragmented first-, third-, and proprietary datasets so teams can turn behavioral and attitudinal insight into campaigns. The Stagwell ID Graph covers **260 million US consumers** and **123 million US households**. A Differential Privacy Vault (Harvard OpenDP) lets teams activate sensitive datasets without exposing individual identities.

### Feature lines (product page)
- Build high-intent audiences from Stagwell proprietary datasets
- Transform seed segments into high-fidelity lookalikes
- Pipe audience insights into activation platforms
- Track and optimize cross-channel performance in real time with secure AI
